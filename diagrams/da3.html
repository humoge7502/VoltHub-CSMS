DA3 · section 29

Outbox pipeline — Oracle OLTP into TimescaleDB OLAP

Every meter tick is written twice inside one transaction: the billing row that decides money, and an outbox row that decides analytics. A relay drains the outbox into the TimescaleDB sink, where the primary key (session_id, ts) makes a replay harmless — at-least-once delivery plus an idempotent sink.

Killed between the COPY and the commit? The marks roll back, the events stay unprocessed, and the sink dedupes them on the primary key when the relay picks them up again. Nothing is lost, nothing is counted twice, and the analytics side never holds a lock the money path needs.

TimescaleDB schema — hypertables, aggregates, policies

why this shape

Chunk keys are time, so every dashboard query touches a bounded set of chunks instead of the whole history. Aggregates are incremental, so a refresh costs the new buckets only. And because the sink dedupes on the primary key, the relay can replay a batch without a reconciliation job.
